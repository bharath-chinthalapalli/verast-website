About Company
VERAST Information Technology, headquartered in Abu Dhabi, is a leading provider of end-to-end ICT solutions and consultancy services across various sectors in Abu Dhabi. The company offers expert support in IT system integration and infrastructure environment setup, serving clients of all sizes. With a strong focus on client needs, VERAST ensures SLA compliance and world-class service delivery.VERAST is a subsidiary company of Digital Compatibility Foundation (DCF), a Saudi-based company, ensuring robust infrastructure and reliable performance.
Its experienced engineers manage complex ICT challenges, while the company continuously enhances quality, efficiency, and innovation. At VERAST Information Technology, we are committed to delivering a high level of safety and security across all sectors, including government, private, and non-profit organizations. We take great care in protecting our clients' sensitive information and do not share any data with other clients. Customer service is the core of our operations, enabling clients to focus on their core business while leaving the complexities of ICT to a trusted partner.
